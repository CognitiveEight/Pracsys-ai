This case is about the governance, financial management, and election disputes of Punjabi Bagh Club (Regd.), a society registered on 27/10/1966 under the Societies Registration Act XXI of 1860. The matter involves challenges to the transparency of the election process and the validity of amended bylaws approved during an Annual General Meeting held on 21/12/2025. Naresh Kumar Gupta and other petitioners have filed W.P.(C) 239/2026 in the High Court of Delhi, along with CM APPL. 1111/2026 and CM APPL. 1112/2026, seeking directions to ensure free and fair elections. Previously, in W.P.(C) 6325/2019, the court had appointed Justice Vinod Goel as an Observer for the elections held on 29/12/2019, where Gurinder Pal Singh (Raju) was elected as President, Satish Jindal as Hony. Gen. Secretary, and Pankaj Arora as Hony. Treasurer. Financial statements for the year ending 31/03/2024, audited by M/s B. D. Mittal & Co., show a Capital Account balance of Rs. 374,481,872.67 and a net deficit of Rs. 17,160,342.82. The current dispute arises from the Election Notification dated 22/12/2025, which set the 2026 election schedule, leading to allegations of unfair practices due to the involvement of sitting Management Committee members, Virender Garg and Arjinder Singh Vohra, in the Election Committee. Although these members reportedly resigned in November 2025, the petitioners maintain that the process remains vitiated. On 08/01/2026, Justice Amit Bansal declined to stay the elections but made the results subject to the outcome of the writ petition. The petitioners seek to enforce democratic principles and ensure that the Registrar of Societies performs its statutory duty to oversee the society's functions. The matter involves various stakeholders and vendors including Prime Furniture, Purshotam Profiles P Ltd, Right Secure P Ltd, S G Hospitality, and United Spirits Ltd. Banking relationships are maintained with Bank of Baroda, Axis Bank Limited, Punjab National Bank, State Bank of India, IDFC First Bank, and Yes Bank Ltd. Key individuals associated with the club's history and current disputes include Surinder Kumar Sachdeva, Rajni Kant Sahni, Joginder Singh Gahlot, Daljit Singh Bindra, Satinder Pal Malhotra, Arushi Ahuja, Ramesh Chander, Manjinder Singh Sirsa, Yogesh Berry, G.C.Sood, Abhishek Kaushik, Sumit K. Batra, D.D. Suri, and Panna Lal. Significant dates in the timeline include 10/12/2019 and the projected completion of certain administrative terms by 01/12/2025.
